## Gotchas & dead ends

- **The issue's own diagnosis was wrong twice, and only running things caught it.** #51 claimed
  `roadmap_guard.py` is single-cursor; it is already per-version — `parse_index` computed a
  `cursor` that `check()` discarded (`_cursor`), and that dead variable is the entire reason the
  code *read* as single-cursor. Removed. #50 claimed the SHOWCASE SAMPLE banners mark what to
  strip; only two files carry that banner. Both premises were plausible from reading the source.
- **"Append-only" is a discipline, not something git knows.** Two branches appending to the Log
  conflict — same insertion point. Nothing is lost (both sides survive), but the State hunk and
  the append-only hunk need *opposite* resolutions, and that asymmetry is the whole constraint.
- **My own first draft of the surface doc violated its own rule** — `agent-runnable` rows with an
  empty *last-run*. Fixed by making `—` mean "claimed, not proved" explicitly, and by actually
  proving the two rows that could be proved here.
- **Both provable checks turned out to report rather than gate.** `asset_integrity.py` never
  vetoes (advisory by contract) and `contrast.py` exits 0 on a *failing* pair. A caller reading
  exit status alone records a pass that never happened — a subtler form of the durable defect
  class, now a rule in the surface doc.
- `example-project/.claude/skills/` was the one layer with no `README.md`; `init-project`'s copy
  step tripped over it. Added.
